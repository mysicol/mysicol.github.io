About

I am Abby Hatcher, a prospective Ph.D. student. I intend to study topics in formal logic, type theory, and the philosophy of mathematics. My specific interests within these areas are diverse.

I attended Drexel University from Fall 2021 to Spring 2026, majoring in Mathematics and Computer Science and minoring in Physics. Throughout this time, I wrote software for neuroengineering research at a Drexel lab and co-oped twice at a small, research-oriented magnetometer company. I was also a course assistant for a wide variety of computer science courses and a math tutor through Drexel's College of Computing and Informatics (or maybe you know it by its new name ... "SCIS").

I am interested in reading and learning about consciousness, metaphysics, and ethics, though these are not my personal areas of expertise. My hobbies include rock climbing, singing, and music production. I love "Story of Your Life" by Ted Chiang.

  • AI consciousness presentation notes, talk for extra credit in CS 380, Jan. 2024

    These are my notes for a short presentation on whether LLMs could possibly be conscious, which I presented soon after the first intelligent-seeming OpenAI GPT models became widely publicized. I evaluated their behaviors, comparing them with the definition of consciousness given by the Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy. (This presentation was a lot more novel and interesting back then than it would be now, and my opinions on this matter are also currently more well-formed.)
